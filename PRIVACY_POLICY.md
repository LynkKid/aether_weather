# Privacy Policy for Aether Weather

**Effective Date:** October 9, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 9, 2026

Welcome to **Aether Weather** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your data is handled. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your rights when using our mobile application **Aether Weather** (the "Application").

By using the Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Location Data
- **Types of Location Data:** Approximate location (`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`) and Precise location (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`).
- **How We Use It:** We use your device's location solely while the Application is in use (foreground) to fetch and display local weather forecasts, hourly/weekly predictions, atmospheric metrics, and severe weather alerts for your immediate area.
- **Background Location:** **We do NOT collect, access, or track your location in the background** when the application is closed or minimized.
- **Retention & Sharing:** Your coordinates (latitude and longitude) are sent ephemerally over encrypted channels (HTTPS) to public weather data providers to retrieve meteorological forecasts. We do not store your location history, create location profiles, or sell your location data to any third party or advertiser.

### B. Notification Permissions
- **Permission (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** If you grant permission, the Application delivers local notifications regarding severe weather alerts, critical atmospheric conditions, and scheduled forecast summaries.
- **Control:** You can enable or disable notifications at any time in the Application's settings or through your device's system settings.

### C. Device & Usage Preferences
- We store user preferences locally on your device using `shared_preferences` (e.g., favorite saved cities, unit preferences such as Celsius/Fahrenheit, metric/imperial, theme selection).
- This data remains strictly on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

### D. Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do **not** require user accounts, logins, or passwords.
- We do **not** collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
- We do **not** collect financial information or payment card data.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services & Data Transmission

The Application interacts with meteorological data APIs (such as Open-Meteo or related weather service endpoints) to obtain accurate atmospheric data.
- Only geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) required for the forecast are sent to these services.
- All communications are secured using industry-standard **HTTPS (Transport Layer Security / TLS)** encryption.
- These providers process requests in an anonymous, aggregated manner and do not associate requests with individual user identities.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

- Because we do not operate a remote user database storing your personal data, we do not retain your historical data on remote servers.
- Any saved preferences (such as saved locations and temperature units) can be completely removed at any time by clearing the Application's cache/storage or uninstalling the Application from your device.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

Aether Weather does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The Application is designed for general audiences as a utility tool. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

---

## 5. Security of Your Information

We prioritize the security of your data. The Application does not transmit sensitive personal identifiers, and all network calls for weather forecasts are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS protocol to prevent unauthorized interception.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or relevant legal requirements. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Any updates become effective immediately upon posting.
